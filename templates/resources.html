🌱 Mental Health Tips

Practice self-compassion

Talk to yourself the way you'd talk to a friend. You're doing better than you think.

Stay connected

Even a short message to one person you trust can lift the weight. Reach out today.

Move your body

A 10-minute walk can shift your mood. Movement releases tension and clears your head.

Sleep matters

Most adults need 7–9 hours. Try a consistent bedtime and limit screens before bed.

Small wins count

Make your bed. Drink water. Step outside. Tiny actions build momentum on hard days.

Limit comparisons

Social media shows highlights, not reality. Mute accounts that drain you.

🧘 Stress Management

4-7-8 breathing

Inhale for 4, hold for 7, exhale for 8. Repeat four times to calm your nervous system.

The 5-4-3-2-1 grounding

Name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.

Box breathing

Inhale 4 — hold 4 — exhale 4 — hold 4. A favorite of Navy SEALs and busy students alike.

Journaling

Write for 5 minutes without editing. Get the worry out of your head onto paper.
